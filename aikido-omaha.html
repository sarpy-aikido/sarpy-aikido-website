Aikido in Omaha, Nebraska

Fifteen minutes from midtown. On the mat since 1998.

Sarpy Aikido serves the Omaha metro from our dojo in La Vista, just south of the city line. We teach one art, we teach it carefully, and we have been doing it in the same community since 1998.

Getting to the Dojo from Omaha

We train at 8116 Park View Blvd in La Vista, NE 68128. La Vista sits directly south of Omaha in Sarpy County, so for most of the metro the drive is shorter than people expect, often shorter than crossing Omaha itself.

From Downtown & Midtown Omaha

Take I-80 west to the 84th Street exit and head south. Roughly 15–20 minutes outside of rush hour.

From West Omaha & Millard

Head east to 84th Street. For much of Millard this is a 10–15 minute drive with no interstate at all.

From Bellevue & Offutt AFB

West on Highway 370 through Papillion. Around 15 minutes. We have trained service members and military families throughout the dojo's history.

From Papillion, Ralston & Gretna

All within about 10–15 minutes. Ralston and Papillion are our immediate neighbors in Sarpy County.

Parking is free and on-site. See the map on our home page for exact directions.

What Aikido Training Involves

The Omaha metro has a deep and varied martial arts community, and we are glad to be part of it. If aikido is the art that has caught your interest, here is what training with us actually looks like, so you know what to expect before you walk in.

Built around redirection

Aikido works by taking an attacker's balance and structure, blending with force and redirecting it rather than meeting it head-on. The goal is to neutralize a situation without needing to be bigger or stronger than the person in front of you.

You train with a partner, not against an opponent

Practice is cooperative by design. You and your partner take turns giving and receiving technique, which lets both of you work at full commitment while staying safe. Saturday open mats add progressive resistance so you can pressure-test what you have learned.

A practice you can keep for decades

Because aikido relies on timing and structure rather than speed or strength, it is something people continue for a very long time. It is common for students to train for decades and still be refining the same fundamentals they learned in their first month.

Every experience level on the same mat

We group by experience. Fundamentals gives newcomers a dedicated place to start, and the Advanced class is where that foundation gets sharpened. Both bring together students at every stage, so beginners train alongside people who have already walked that path.

Common Questions from Omaha Students

How far is Sarpy Aikido from Omaha?

Our dojo is at 8116 Park View Blvd in La Vista, immediately south of the Omaha city line. From midtown the drive is typically 15–20 minutes, and from west Omaha or Millard it is often closer to 10–15.

Do I need any experience to start aikido?

No. Our Fundamentals class on Mondays and Thursdays at 6:00 PM is built for people with no martial arts background at all. Most of our students started that way.

Am I too old to start aikido?

Almost certainly not. Aikido does not rely on speed or strength, and we have students who began in their forties and fifties. Tell us about any injuries and we will work around them.

Can I watch an aikido class before joining?

Yes. You are welcome to come watch any scheduled class, or call (402) 981-7096 ahead if you would rather talk first.
